🚚 Supply Chain & Logistics Performance Analysis

📌 Project Overview

In the fast-paced world of logistics, efficiency is the difference between profit and loss. This project transforms raw supply chain data into a comprehensive Power BI Dashboard, providing actionable insights into warehouse operations, inventory health, and cost distribution.

The goal is to bridge the gap between technical data engineering and strategic business decision-making, helping stakeholders minimize operational bottlenecks and maximize flow.

🛠️ Technical Stack

Power BI: Main tool for interactive data visualization and storytelling.

Power Query: Used for ETL processes, data cleaning, and attribute transformation.

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Created complex measures for KPIs like DSI, Inventory Turnover, and Utilization rates.

📊 Key Insights & KPIs

Warehouse Efficiency: Monitored the Warehouse Utilization Rate (currently at 34.08%) to identify space optimization opportunities.

Inventory Health: Engineered logic to calculate Days Sales of Inventory (DSI) and Inventory Turnover Ratio to track how fast stock is moving.

Logistics Cost Analysis: Visualized transportation costs by region and category, pinpointing the West region as a high-cost area.

Operational Flow: Analyzed Lead Times (Avg ~15.8 days) and Backorder Status (248 Pending vs. 114 Canceled) to highlight fulfillment friction.

Sales Trends: Tracked growth patterns from 2020 to 2024, identifying significant recovery and seasonal peaks.

💡 Strategic Recommendations

Based on the data findings, the following actions are recommended:

Monetize Underutilized Space: With utilization at 34%, the business should consider "Storage-as-a-Service" models or consolidating inventory to reduce overhead.

Supplier Management: Focus on the Accessories category, which exhibits the highest lead times, to improve fulfillment speed.

Automated Reordering: Use the Inventory Turnover Ratio to automate reorder points, reducing the 248 pending backorders and preventing customer churn.

Regional Negotiation: Re-evaluate carrier contracts in the West region to bring transportation costs in line with other territories.
